# High-ambition journal routing

## Purpose

Aim high without treating journal prestige as a writing style. Select the highest venue
whose scope, article policy, evidence standard, and readership genuinely match the review,
then preserve a scientifically coherent transfer path.

## Build a three-level ladder

Use live official sources to define:

- **Stretch:** the highest scientifically defensible venue, with a credible editorial
  reason to own the review.
- **Best fit:** the venue whose normal readership and article mix most closely match the
  completed synthesis.
- **Floor:** a reputable specialist or application venue that still values the review's
  central contribution.

Do not use quartile, impact factor, or publisher brand as the only selection criterion.
Record the date of every policy check because scope and review eligibility can change.

## Pass the eligibility gate first

Before journal-specific rewriting, verify:

1. whether reviews are accepted, invited, proposed, commissioned, or restricted to
   priority topics;
2. whether a presubmission proposal is required;
3. review article type, abstract and keyword limits, highlights, graphical abstract,
   data statement, supplementary material, and AI-disclosure requirements;
4. whether the manuscript's subject and scale are explicitly in scope;
5. whether a current special issue changes the normal eligibility route.

When an invitation or proposal is required, prepare a short editorial package containing:

- provisional title and 200鈥?50-word abstract;
- one-sentence novelty claim;
- a table of the closest reviews from the previous two to three years;
- proposed contents and visual programme;
- why the journal's readership needs the synthesis;
- relevant expertise of the author team;
- expected submission date.

Do not submit the full manuscript through a normal route when the official page says that
reviews require invitation or prior approval.

## Select a synthesis profile

### Mechanism-to-performance

Use when the journal rewards material-scale explanation.

Require:

- explicit descriptors and comparison units;
- causal links from chemistry, morphology, dispersion, hydration, or interface to
  engineering response;
- triangulation across complementary methods;
- distinction between observation, supported inference, hypothesis, and speculation;
- author-original mechanism or evidence maps;
- quantitative normalization where defensible;
- tight limits on field or sustainability claims not connected to material-scale evidence.

### Application-to-performance

Use when the journal primarily serves construction-material practice.

Require:

- processing and mixture-design windows;
- fresh, mechanical, durability, and constructability consequences;
- scale, field, standard, cost, and supply constraints;
- photographs only when they document application readiness or failure;
- decision matrices with validation and stop criteria;
- clear separation of laboratory feasibility from service-life validation.

### Evidence-to-design

Use when the review's central contribution is a reproducibility, reporting, or
decision-making framework.

Require:

- an auditable evidence matrix;
- normalization of dosage, water, age, control, and test basis;
- an evidence-maturity or contradiction map;
- minimum reporting requirements;
- experimental stop criteria;
- a staged research programme that can falsify the review's synthesis.

Use a hybrid only when every main section advances the same causal thesis.

## Upgrade the manuscript, not its adjectives

For the stretch profile, test whether the manuscript contributes at least two of:

- a new comparison unit;
- a verified and reusable dataset;
- a defensible quantitative synthesis;
- reconciliation of a central contradiction;
- an evidence-linked mechanism framework;
- a design or reporting framework with stop criteria;
- a scale-up or service-life bridge supported by evidence.

If it contributes only breadth or a later search date, redesign the question or lower the
target. Do not compensate with words such as `comprehensive`, `novel`, `robust`, or
`state-of-the-art`.

## High-ambition visual gate

Every retained figure must either supply evidence or perform synthesis. Under the stretch
profile:

- include at least one author-original figure that changes comparison, mechanism,
  uncertainty, or decision logic;
- prefer normalized plots, causal evidence chains, competing hypotheses, and design
  windows over decorative collages;
- treat photographs as scale or failure evidence, not prestige signals;
- distinguish direct evidence from hypotheses by line style, colour, symbol, or caption;
- remove low-resolution inherited figures when their information can be redrawn lawfully
  and more transparently;
- preserve original observations and permissions when the source image itself is evidence.

The first scientific figure should announce the manuscript's contribution, not merely
illustrate the material class.

## Desk-rejection simulation

Answer each question with `pass`, `revise`, or `stop`:

- Is the article type currently eligible?
- Can the novelty be explained without saying only `comprehensive` or `recent`?
- Does the title signal the synthesis axis?
- Does the abstract state conclusions, boundaries, and the practical or scientific
  consequence?
- Is the review method named accurately and reproducible to the level claimed?
- Are decisive claims grounded in primary evidence?
- Does at least one figure or table contain knowledge created by the review?
- Are quantitative comparisons normalized or explicitly declared incompatible?
- Are connected research programmes distinguished from independent replication?
- Are uncertainty, negative results, and limitations visible?
- Does the conclusion answer the review questions without exceeding the evidence?

Stop journal-specific packaging when eligibility, novelty, or method-label accuracy fails.

## Plan the transfer before submission

Record how the best-fit and floor versions would change:

- title and abstract emphasis;
- mechanism versus application depth;
- figures retained, redrawn, moved to supplementary material, or deleted;
- tables and data package;
- scale-up, durability, and standards discussion;
- word count and section compression;
- cover-letter rationale.

Do not degrade scientific integrity during transfer. Preserve verified references, evidence
classes, water and dosage bases, figure provenance, and explicit limitations.
