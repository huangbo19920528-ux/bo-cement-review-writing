# Manuscript architecture

## Build sections around questions

Every main section should answer a question that advances the review argument. A useful
sequence moves from identity and processing to mechanisms, performance, comparison, and
translation.

## Paragraph test

Keep a subsection only when it contains enough evidence for:

1. a claim or comparison;
2. supporting evidence;
3. boundary conditions or disagreement;
4. a synthesis or implication.

Merge a subsection that consists of one or two short descriptive paragraphs.

## Introduction

Use four functions:

1. establish the engineering problem;
2. define why existing comparisons are inadequate;
3. identify the precise review gap;
4. state the review questions and contribution.

Distribute citations by claim. Do not attach a large citation cluster to a paragraph of
several unrelated ideas.

## Review method

Report:

- local corpus construction or PDF-processing route when used;
- databases and search date;
- complete concepts or queries;
- date and language limits;
- inclusion and exclusion rules;
- screening and deduplication;
- update search;
- evidence-extraction unit;
- synthesis method;
- backward and forward citation-chaining method when used;
- why meta-analysis was or was not appropriate.

Keep final source counts synchronized with the abstract, flow figure, methods, and reference
list.

## Results and synthesis

Use material–mixture–processing combinations as comparison units. For each domain:

- establish the relevant descriptors;
- compare direction and magnitude;
- expose water, dispersion, air, binder, curing, and testing confounders;
- reconcile disagreements;
- state confidence and limitations.

## Research agenda

Replace “more research is needed” with staged questions, minimum deliverables, failure
criteria, and evidence motivating each stage.

## Journal expression

Use the target journal's current author guide and recent accepted papers to normalize title
structure, abstract density, headings, captions, units, abbreviations, and references. Learn
disciplinary phrasing without copying sentences. Do not mechanically force passive voice;
prefer precise, concise, evidence-linked prose.

## Author closing pass

Require the author to:

- revise hard or generic transitions;
- state an original, defensible judgement on central controversies;
- recheck claim-level citations and page locations;
- approve the novelty claim, limitations, and conclusion scope;
- remove any wording that implies the AI performed experiments or made scholarly decisions.

## Shortening pass

Remove:

- generic textbook background;
- repeated definitions;
- repeated mechanism explanations;
- paper-by-paper lists without synthesis;
- decorative figures;
- tables that duplicate prose;
- speculative future applications without a validation route.
