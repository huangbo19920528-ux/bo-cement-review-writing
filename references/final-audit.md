# Final audit

## Scientific integrity

- [ ] Every DOI and bibliographic record was verified.
- [ ] Every numerical claim matches its cited source and comparison basis.
- [ ] Direct observations and inferred mechanisms use different language.
- [ ] Connected papers are not presented as independent replication.
- [ ] Confounders and negative or null findings are represented.
- [ ] Conclusions do not exceed the reviewed materials, ages, or exposure conditions.

## Scope and method

- [ ] The novelty claim remains defensible after the final search update.
- [ ] Search date, databases, queries, filters, and exclusions are reported.
- [ ] Source counts match everywhere.
- [ ] The evidence-extraction and synthesis methods are clear.
- [ ] “Systematic” is used only when justified.

## Structure and prose

- [ ] Each main section answers a scientific question.
- [ ] Thin subsections have been merged.
- [ ] Paragraphs contain claim, evidence, boundary, and synthesis.
- [ ] Citation piles and repetitive “X et al.” sentences have been revised.
- [ ] Generic, duplicated, and low-value text has been removed.

## Tables

- [ ] Every evidence row has a reference.
- [ ] Reported findings are distinguished from review interpretation.
- [ ] Units, dosage basis, water treatment, age, and controls are clear.
- [ ] No arbitrary scores appear without an auditable rule.
- [ ] Tables remain readable at final page width.

## Figures

- [ ] Every figure has a unique role.
- [ ] No panel, crop, or photograph is repeated.
- [ ] Panel labels are consistent and at the upper left.
- [ ] Panel descriptions are in captions.
- [ ] No inherited label, legend, or text is clipped.
- [ ] Fonts, line weights, colours, and border treatments are coherent.
- [ ] Micrographs retain scale bars and necessary annotations.
- [ ] Every external panel has a source and acceptable permission status.
- [ ] Distinct studies or programmes are not presented as paired experiments.

## Numbering and references

- [ ] Figures, tables, equations, and references are sequential.
- [ ] Every in-text citation resolves to a reference.
- [ ] Every reference is cited.
- [ ] Captions and cross-references use the final numbering.
- [ ] Reference formatting is internally consistent.

## Rendered-page inspection

- [ ] Every page was rendered and inspected.
- [ ] No image, equation, caption, table, or reference is clipped.
- [ ] Figures and captions remain together where practical.
- [ ] Subscripts, superscripts, symbols, and units render correctly.
- [ ] Body text is black, justified when appropriate, and uses the requested font and spacing.
- [ ] Blank space is intentional rather than caused by a broken object.

## Final comparison

- [ ] The complete manuscript was compared with benchmark reviews.
- [ ] Its evidence density, critical depth, table utility, and figure quality are competitive.
- [ ] Differences from benchmarks are scientifically justified.
- [ ] Internal drafting notes, version labels, and instructions to authors were removed.
